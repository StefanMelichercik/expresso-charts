The app keeps your data on your computer

  • Your charts, layouts, settings and backups are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows' own protection. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Screenshots you import are processed locally and are not sent anywhere.
  • To place charts, the app reads the position and title of poker windows on your screen. This stays on your computer.
  • Licences are tied to a machine code: a one-way fingerprint of your Windows installation. It contains no personal information.
  • A diagnostics file is only created when you click “Save diagnostics”, and only reaches us if you email it yourself. It contains no charts.
  • The app checks a public file on GitHub to see if a newer version exists and, when you choose to update, downloads the new version from GitHub. These requests contain no information about you; GitHub may log your IP address as with any website visit (see GitHub's privacy statement).

When you buy

Purchases are handled by Gumroad as Merchant of Record. They process your name, email, billing address and payment details under their own privacy policy. We receive your name, email, country, order details and the machine code you enter at checkout, so we can deliver and support your licence.

When you email us

We use your email and message only to answer you and keep a record of support and licences issued.

This website

This website uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security.

How long we keep data

Order and licence records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting rules, then deleted. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it (unless the law requires us to keep it), and you can complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the address above.
